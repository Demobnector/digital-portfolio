About

Zeejin Guevarra Ganaban

I am Zeejin Guevarra Ganaban, a third-year BSIT student at City College of Calamba and a digital artist exploring programming, web development, game development, graphic design, and 2D animation. I began seriously experimenting with digital art in 2022 and have continued developing both technical and creative skills through academic, personal, and collaborative work.

I enjoy building things with code while also communicating ideas visually through illustration and animation. My goal is to keep improving across technology and creative media, and to turn long-term dedication into work I can be proud to share.
